SkuHeal is a mineralized-collagen skull defect repair plug from Allgens Medical (China), used in neurosurgery to fill small-area skull bone defects. It is classified as a class D medical device and holds its own circulation registration certificate, separate from the BONGOLD line's registration.

Detailed indications, contraindications and usage instructions are provided in the manufacturer's documentation, intended for healthcare professionals and professional partners.
