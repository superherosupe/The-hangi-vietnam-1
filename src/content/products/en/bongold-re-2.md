BONGOLD Re-2 is a medium-hardness blocks artificial bone graft material from Allgens Medical (China), synthesized from mineralized collagen that mimics the structure of natural bone. It is classified as a class D medical device and holds a circulation registration certificate issued by Vietnam's Ministry of Health.

Detailed indications, contraindications and usage instructions are provided in the manufacturer's documentation, intended for healthcare professionals and professional partners.
